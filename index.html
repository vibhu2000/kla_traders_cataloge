Frequently Asked Questions

How to place an order?

To place an order, please send us the product you are interested in or a reference image. We work exclusively on a made-to-order basis; to initiate your order, a 50% deposit is required in advance.

How long does it take to fulfill an order?

Depending on the complexity, we generally take 2 to 4 days to fulfill your order.

Can we customize a product, or are they only available in standard sizes?

While most of our products are available in standard specifications, we do offer customization for select items. If you have a specific request, please contact us with the details and we'll be happy to help you.

Payment methods?

We accept Online/UPI/Net Banking/Cheque. You must pay a 50% deposit to get started.

Deliveries and Shipping?

We offer fast, reliable shipping. Standard shipping typically takes 2-3 business days. Shipping rates are calculated based on order weight and destination

What materials do you use?

Our products are available in a wide range of materials to suit different industrial and commercial needs. We stock Mild Steel(MS), Stainless Steel(SS), High Tensile(HT) and galvanized finishes.
